01 · Collect

A signal appears.

Professional crawl, local, search, content, conversion, and AI-readiness checks bring the evidence into one view. A warning is now visible—but it has not earned action yet.

02 · Validate

Evidence challenges the noise.

SiteChase reproduces the issue, removes false positives, and identifies missing business facts. The signal becomes an observed defect, a supported opportunity, an open question, or noise.

03 · Prioritize

The queue gets shorter—and more useful.

Business relevance, evidence strength, effort, urgency, and risk shape the recommendation. The loudest alert does not automatically become the next move.

04 · Approve

A person owns the decision.

AI can investigate quickly and organize evidence. It does not get the final vote. The recommendation moves only after approval, revision, or a deliberate hold.

The decision—not another dashboard

Fewer recommendations. Clearer reasons.

SEO tools are good at finding more. A business needs help deciding what matters now. SiteChase is designed to carry the reasoning forward: what was observed, what supports it, what the proposed action is meant to accomplish, and what could go wrong.

The result is a practical queue. Strong evidence rises. Uncertain findings wait for context. Repetitive noise stays out of the way.

What you receive

A practical report—not a pile of exports.

The audit is designed to show the first fixes that matter, not overwhelm you with every possible technical detail. It separates observation, inference, recommendation, approval, and verification so you can see both the opportunity and the limits of the evidence.

Is this an autonomous SEO platform?

No. This is a concept for a broader recurring intelligence workflow SiteChase is developing and piloting, not a finished self-service platform.

Does SiteChase automatically change a live website?

No. Scope and implementation are agreed before any live change, and the model shown here keeps an explicit approval point.

Are the findings and scores on this page real?

No. Every example in this preview is synthetic and exists only to explain the decision process.

Does this guarantee rankings, traffic, leads, or AI citations?

No. SiteChase can improve the quality and traceability of decisions; it cannot guarantee a specific search outcome.
