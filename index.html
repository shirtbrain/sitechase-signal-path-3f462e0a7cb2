Straight answers

What this concept is—and is not.

Is this an autonomous SEO platform?

No. This page is a concept for a broader recurring intelligence workflow SiteChase is developing and piloting. It does not represent a finished self-service product.

Does SiteChase automatically change a live website?

No. The model shown here keeps an explicit approval point. Scope and implementation would be agreed before any live change.

Are the findings and scores on this page real?

No. Every example on this preview is synthetic and exists only to explain the decision process.

Does this guarantee rankings, traffic, leads, or AI citations?

No. Search outcomes depend on many factors. SiteChase can improve the quality and traceability of decisions; it cannot guarantee a specific result.
